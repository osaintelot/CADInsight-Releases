CAD Insight Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · CAD Auto Trims LLC

This policy explains what the CAD Insight Android app ("the app") and the CAD Insight timer unit ("the unit") do with your information. CAD Insight is made by CAD Auto Trims LLC ("we", "us").

The short version: your runs, videos and settings stay on your phone and your unit. We have no user accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and we never sell your data.

1. Information that stays on your devices

  • Run data. Times, speeds, GPS traces (latitude, longitude, altitude), notes, tags and personal bests are recorded by the unit and copied to the app. They are stored on the unit's memory card and in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not receive them.
  • Unit connection. The app connects directly to your unit over the unit's own Wi-Fi network. This connection never goes through the internet. Saved unit names and Wi-Fi details stay on your phone.
  • Settings are stored on the unit and on your phone.

2. Camera and microphone

If you turn on run video, the app uses your phone's camera and microphone to record clips around your runs. Clips are saved on your phone (in the app's storage, and in your Movies/CAD Insight folder when you save or export one). Clips are never uploaded by the app. You decide whether to share a clip, using your phone's normal share options. You can deny camera and microphone access; the timer works without them.

3. Weather lookup (optional)

To correct run times for weather, the app can look up the temperature, humidity and air pressure at the time and place of a run. To do this, it sends the run's approximate location (from the unit's GPS) and date and time to Open-Meteo, a weather data service. No name, account or device identifier is sent with it. Open-Meteo's handling of this request is covered by its own terms and privacy policy.

You can turn this off in the app's Settings, so that no location is ever sent. You can still enter weather by hand.

4. Diagnostic reports

If you tap Run diagnostic and then Send report, the app creates a plain text report: the unit's name and firmware version, the app version, your phone model and Android version, and the test results (such as GPS, memory card and clock status). The report contains no run data, location or videos. It is sent only if you choose to send it, using the app you pick (for example email). We use it only to help you fix the problem.

5. Updates

To check for unit firmware updates, the app downloads update files from our public download page on GitHub. Like any web request, this exposes your phone's IP address to GitHub. No personal information is sent.

6. Purchases

If you buy add-ons such as themes, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. To unlock an add-on on your unit, we receive Google Play's purchase confirmation and your unit's ID, and keep that record so the add-on can be restored if you replace your phone or reinstall the app.

7. What we don't do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no ad or analytics tools.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We do not use your phone's own location. Run locations come from the unit's GPS.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete runs and videos in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes everything in its private storage. Clips you saved to your Movies folder stay there until you delete them. Runs on the unit can be deleted from the unit or by removing its memory card. To ask us to delete a purchase record, email us (see below). Note that we will then be unable to restore that purchase.

9. Children

CAD Insight is a vehicle timing tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Safety and accuracy

Use CAD Insight only where it is legal and safe, such as a closed course or drag strip. Do not operate the app or unit while driving. Obey all traffic laws.

Run times, speeds, distances and corrected figures are estimates calculated from GPS data. Their accuracy depends on satellite reception, signal conditions, mounting and other factors outside our control, and is not guaranteed. Weather-corrected results are also estimates. CAD Insight results are for personal and entertainment use only. They are not official timing and should not be used for racing results, legal or insurance purposes, or any decision where accuracy is critical.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.
